# Smoke grenade comparison (in progress)

Compare the approved pixel-puff look in two bounded, area-owned implementations:

- A small density grid updated at a fixed low rate, swept projectile carving.
- A bounded bag of drifting puffs, pushed or removed by the same effects.

Opt-in prototype first, not a change to enemy visibility, HP, lights or audio.
Grenade endpoint and cloud lifetimes are owned by the currently mounted area.
Native game clips and web/export checks are pending. No runtime claim yet.
